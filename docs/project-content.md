# Project portfolio

The source spreadsheet was the owner's `Project list.xlsx`, `Sheet1!A3:E37`, supplied on 7 November 2026. Its yellow rows for original source numbers 16, 17 and 21 remain excluded. At the owner's request, Villa Luvana (original source 9) and TITAN Mildew Abatement & Humidity Redesign (original source 24) were also removed. The portfolio now contains 30 retained spreadsheet projects and three added projects: BEEAH Group Headquarters, Al Shera’a DEWA Headquarters and W Hotel Abu Dhabi.

The 33 displayed records are maintained in `src/data/projects.json` in portfolio order. `sourceNumber` is the continuous display order from 1 to 33; `originalSourceNumber` preserves each spreadsheet number, and is `null` for the three added projects. Every project has an ID, name, scope, client, location (blank when unavailable) and responsive image assignment. The full portfolio and homepage selections read this one array. Individual project routes are not used; all project cards remain on `/projects/` and homepage selections link to the portfolio. There are no completed/ongoing categories. Existing `/completed/` and `/on-going/` paths resolve to Projects with its canonical URL and permanent redirect rules.

Project names, scopes, clients and locations retain source details except for the requested corrections: Khazna DXB3 Data Centre - Ibn Battuta and Dubai Holding Head Quarters, Dubai. The three appended records are BEEAH Group Headquarters (Seasonal commissioning for LEED; BEEAH Group; Sharjah), Al Shera’a DEWA Headquarters (CAR Park Ventilation Smoke Testing; DEWA; Al Jaddaf, Dubai), and W Hotel Abu Dhabi (Ventilation System Re-Balancing; W Hotel Abu Dhabi; Yas Island, Abu Dhabi). The BEEAH and DEWA client/location details use the named owner and official headquarters location. Locations were not inferred for older blank spreadsheet cells.

Matching project imagery is used when a suitable source is available. Previous-site photographs for Al Badia, Petalz, MAF Tower 01, Sensoria and Dubai Holding are retained. Official photographs are used for BEEAH Group Headquarters and W Abu Dhabi; the BEEAH exterior is published by BEEAH and credited there to Hufton+Crow, while the W Hotel exterior comes from Marriott's official gallery. The Explorers image is from Majid Al Futtaim's official press release and the Wynn construction photograph is from the official Wynn Al Marjan Island progress page. Golf Hillside and AZHA use official developer renderings from Emaar and Emirates Properties, respectively; they are labelled as renderings, not photographs. The portfolio currently contains nine project photographs, two official developer renderings and 22 generated architectural illustrations. Illustrations are labelled and described as illustrations in alt text; they represent building types and regional context, not verified photographs of the named project. Do not present an illustration as an actual project photo. Replace an illustration with an approved project photo by changing its paths, alt text and `imageKind` to `project`.

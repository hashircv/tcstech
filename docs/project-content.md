# Project portfolio

The current source is the owner's `Project list.xlsx`, `Sheet1!A3:E37`, supplied on 7 November 2026. Its 35 rows replace the previous WordPress project selection. Yellow fill `FFFFFF00` appears in columns B–E of rows 18, 19 and 23; these correspond to source numbers 16, 17 and 21 and are excluded. Source number 6 (Wynn Hotel and Resorts) remains; only the separate Wynn duct-leakage entry at number 21 is removed.

The 32 retained records are in `src/data/projects.json` in spreadsheet order. Each includes the source number, name, scope, client, location and image assignment. Display numbering is continuous. The full portfolio and homepage selections read this same array, so future edits only need one data file. There are no completed/ongoing categories. Existing `/completed/` and `/on-going/` paths resolve to the Projects page with its canonical URL; the build includes their permanent redirect rules for compatible hosts.

Project names, scopes, clients and locations retain the source text after whitespace cleanup. The apostrophe in Al QOU'A uses a standard ASCII apostrophe to avoid a broken character in older imported copies. Locations are blank in E36 and E37 and are omitted from those cards. The Sensoria name contains JBR while its location cell E30 says JVC; this source inconsistency is preserved for owner review rather than guessed away.

Matching project imagery is used where a suitable source is available. The previous-site photographs for Al Badia, Petalz, MAF Tower 01, Sensoria and Dubai Holding are retained. The Explorers image is from Majid Al Futtaim's official press release and the Wynn construction photograph is from the official Wynn Al Marjan Island progress page. Golf Hillside and AZHA use official developer renderings from Emaar and Emirates Properties, respectively; they are labelled as renderings, not photographs. The remaining 23 records have distinct generated architectural illustrations, each visibly labelled and described as an illustration in its alt text. They represent building types and regional context, not the real appearance of the named projects. Generated images contain no employees or TCS-branded equipment. Do not replace this distinction with claims of authentic site photography. Replace an illustration with an approved project photo by changing its paths, alt text and `imageKind` to `project`.

Current first-party image source references (accessed 8 November 2026): [Emaar Golf Hillside](https://www.emaar.com/en/properties/golf-hillside-at-dubai-hills-estate), [Emirates Properties AZHA Community](https://www.epgroup.ae/azha-community-ajman-uae/), [Wynn Al Marjan Island progress updates](https://wynnalmarjanisland.com/progress-updates), and [Majid Al Futtaim Explorers announcement](https://www.majidalfuttaim.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/detail/2026/07/majid-al-futtaim-officially-opens-explorers--a-new-era-of-family-entertainment-with-immersive-free-play-and-workshops-all-under-one-roof). Their locally stored derivatives are cropped and optimized WebP versions. Verify usage rights with the respective rights holders before commercial publication.

The new imagery was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The common prompt and each scene prompt are recorded in `project-image-prompts.json`. Generated assets are 1000px and 640px WebP files at quality 82, using descriptive filenames under `public/images/projects/`. Retained photographs keep their native dimensions; `imageWidth`, `imageHeight` and `mobileWidth` record the actual sizes for accurate responsive selection without upscaling. Authoring PNGs stay outside the deployable repository. Old unused project images and the obsolete status-based PDF lists were removed.

Verification covers all 32 source records and their exact order, exclusion of the three yellow rows, unique image assignments, responsive loading, visible image provenance labels, homepage order and legacy route metadata.
